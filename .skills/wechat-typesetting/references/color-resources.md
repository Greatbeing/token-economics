## Recommended Color Palette Websites

### 1. LOLColors - Designer's Private Color Cards
**URL:** https://www.webdesignrankings.com/resources/lolcolors/

**Best for:** Quick color inspiration with pre-made palettes
**Features:**
- Curated color combinations
- Copy hex codes with one click
- Minimalist interface
- Great for social media content

**Use case:** When you need a cohesive 3-4 color palette fast

---

### 2. Chinese Traditional Colors - Palace Aesthetics
**URL:** https://color.uisdc.com

**Best for:** Elegant, culturally-rich color schemes
**Features:**
- Traditional Chinese color names and stories
- Low saturation, sophisticated tones
- Perfect for literary and cultural content
- Unique color identities (e.g., 豆绿, 月白, 胭脂)

**Use case:** Accounts focused on traditional culture, art, or upscale lifestyle

**Popular picks:**
- 月白 (Moonlight White): #D6ECF0
- 豆绿 (Bean Green): #96CE54
- 胭脂 (Rouge): #9D2933
- 黛蓝 (Indigo Blue): #425066

---

### 3. ColorHunt - Trending Color Combos
**URL:** https://colorhunt.co

**Best for:** Modern, trendy 4-color palettes
**Features:**
- Community-voted trending palettes
- Filter by popularity, date, or random
- Tags for different moods (vintage, neon, pastel, etc.)
- Download palettes in multiple formats

**Use case:** Stay current with design trends, explore diverse styles

**Pro tip:** Sort by "Popular" to see what's working for others right now

## Recommended Color Combinations by Account Type

### Energetic & Motivational Accounts
**Primary colors:** Orange (#FF6B35, #F77F00), Yellow (#FFB800, #FCBF49)
**Accent:** Dark gray (#2B2D42) for contrast
**Example palette:**
```
Primary:   #FF6B35 (Vibrant Orange)
Secondary: #FFE66D (Warm Yellow)
Text:      #3F3F3F (Dark Gray)
Accent:    #FFFFFF (White)
```

---

### Professional & Business Accounts
**Primary colors:** Blue (#1E88E5, #2C73D2), Gray (#607D8B, #78909C), Red (#D32F2F)
**Accent:** White (#FFFFFF) for clean spacing
**Example palette:**
```
Primary:   #1E88E5 (Corporate Blue)
Secondary: #607D8B (Slate Gray)
Text:      #3F3F3F (Dark Gray)
Accent:    #E3F2FD (Light Blue bg)
```

---

### Fresh & Artistic Accounts (Morandi Style)
**Characteristics:** Low saturation, muted tones, soothing aesthetics
**Example palette:**
```
Primary:   #A8DADC (Powder Blue)
Secondary: #E5989B (Dusty Rose)
Text:      #595959 (Soft Gray)
Accent:    #F1FAEE (Cream White)
```

**Note:** Morandi colors have saturation ≤60% and brightness ≤80%

## Color Usage Guidelines

### Primary Color Selection
Choose based on:
1. **Brand identity** - Does your logo have a dominant color?
2. **Content tone** - Serious vs. playful, professional vs. casual
3. **Target audience** - Age, gender, interests
4. **Emotional goal** - Energize, calm, inspire trust, spark joy

### Supporting Colors
- Use sparingly (1-2 max)
- Should complement, not compete with primary
- Often lighter/darker shades of primary or neutral grays

### Text Colors
**Body text:**
- #3F3F3F (softer than pure black, easier to read)
- #595959 (even softer, good for long-form)

**Emphasized text:**
- Use primary brand color + bold
- Limit to ≤3 instances per screen

**Annotations/metadata:**
- #999999 or #AAAAAA (light gray, de-emphasized)

## Quick Color Testing Workflow

1. **Choose palette** from one of the websites above
2. **Apply to sample text** in your editor
3. **Preview on mobile** (colors look different on phone screens!)
4. **Test readability** - Can you read comfortably for 2+ minutes?
5. **Check hierarchy** - Does emphasis stand out without shouting?

**Red flags:**
- Can't read comfortably after 30 seconds → too bright/saturated
- All text looks same importance → not enough contrast
- Feels like a disco → too many colors

## Color Accessibility Tips

1. **Contrast ratio:** Ensure text color vs. background has 4.5:1 ratio minimum
   - Tool: https://webaim.org/resources/contrastchecker/

2. **Avoid red/green only** for emphasis (colorblind users can't distinguish)

3. **Don't rely on color alone** - Use bold, size, or icons too

4. **Test in grayscale** - Your hierarchy should work without color
