## Overview of Recommended Editors

Detailed comparison of 6 typesetting tools for WeChat Official Accounts, helping you choose the right one based on your needs.

---

## 1. 135 Editor (135编辑器) ⭐ Most Popular

**URL:** https://www.135editor.com/

### Strengths
- **Massive template library** - 10,000+ styles covering all industries
- **Full-featured** - Everything you need in one platform
- **Active community** - Constantly updated with new templates
- **AI assistant** - Built-in AI writing and title generation (paid)
- **Style collections** - Save favorite styles for reuse
- **Data sync** - Cloud storage across devices

### Weaknesses
- **Can be overwhelming** - Too many options for beginners
- **Paid features** - Best templates require VIP membership (¥99-299/year)
- **Learning curve** - Takes time to master all features

### Best for
- Professional content creators
- Accounts needing diverse styles
- Users willing to invest in premium features
- Teams managing multiple accounts

### Pricing
- Free tier: Basic templates, daily limits
- VIP: ¥99/year (standard), ¥299/year (premium)

### Unique Features
- **Smart formatting** - Auto-apply consistent styles to entire article
- **Color palette tool** - Extract colors from images
- **Scheduled publishing** - Direct integration with WeChat

---

## 2. Xiumi Editor (秀米编辑器) 🎨 Layout Master

**URL:** https://xiumi.us/

### Strengths
- **Layout-focused** - Best-in-class multi-column layouts
- **Fresh design** - Modern, clean aesthetic
- **Section templates** - Mix-and-match modular components
- **Easy image grids** - Create photo collages effortlessly
- **Responsive design** - Looks great on all screen sizes

### Weaknesses
- **Less traditional styles** - Not ideal for formal/business content
- **Smaller template library** - Compared to 135 Editor
- **Slightly slower** - Interface can lag with complex layouts

### Best for
- Lifestyle and creative content
- Image-heavy articles (travel, food, fashion)
- Users who prioritize visual storytelling
- Accounts with strong aesthetic identity

### Pricing
- Free tier: Full feature access with watermark
- VIP: ¥88/year (removes watermark, premium templates)

### Unique Features
- **Section library** - Pre-designed intro/outro blocks
- **Image filters** - Instagram-style filters built-in
- **Advanced columns** - Up to 5-column layouts

---

## 3. 96 Editor (96编辑器) 💰 Budget Champion

**URL:** https://bj.96weixin.com/

### Strengths
- **Completely free** - No hidden paywalls
- **Simple interface** - Minimal learning curve
- **Solid basics** - All essential formatting tools
- **No ads** - Clean workspace
- **Fast** - Lightweight, quick loading

### Weaknesses
- **Limited templates** - Smaller selection than competitors
- **Basic features** - No AI, advanced layouts, or automation
- **Dated design** - Interface feels older
- **Manual process** - More hands-on work required

### Best for
- Beginners just starting out
- Low-budget operations
- Simple content without complex layouts
- Users who prefer minimal tools

### Pricing
- 100% free

### Unique Features
- **Emoji library** - Large collection of expressive emojis
- **GIF support** - Easy animated GIF insertion

---

## 4. iPaiban Editor (i排版编辑器) 🔧 Power User Tool

**URL:** https://x.ipaiban.com/

### Strengths
- **Advanced formatting** - Granular control over styling
- **CSS customization** - Edit raw styles for unique looks
- **Markdown support** - Write in Markdown, export to rich text
- **Batch operations** - Format multiple articles at once
- **API access** - Integrate with your own tools (paid)

### Weaknesses
- **Steep learning curve** - Not beginner-friendly
- **Technical knowledge** - Best if you understand HTML/CSS
- **Overwhelming options** - Analysis paralysis for simple tasks

### Best for
- Tech-savvy creators
- Developers and designers
- Users with custom branding requirements
- High-volume content operations

### Pricing
- Free tier: Core features
- Pro: ¥199/year (API, batch tools, priority support)

### Unique Features
- **Code editor** - Direct HTML/CSS editing
- **Version control** - Save multiple drafts with history
- **A/B testing** - Compare formatting variations (paid)

---

## 5. Yiban Assistant (壹伴助手) 🚀 Browser Extension

**URL:** https://yiban.io/

### Strengths
- **Native integration** - Works inside WeChat backend directly
- **One-click sync** - No copy-paste needed
- **Data analytics** - View article performance in real-time
- **Reader management** - Track follower interactions
- **Chrome extension** - Lightweight, no separate platform

### Weaknesses
- **Requires browser extension** - Not standalone
- **Limited templates** - Focuses on functionality over design
- **WeChat dependency** - Only works within WeChat ecosystem
- **Privacy concerns** - Third-party access to your account

### Best for
- Users who mainly work in WeChat backend
- Data-driven creators
- Those who hate copy-pasting
- Professional account managers

### Pricing
- Free tier: Basic formatting, limited analytics
- VIP: ¥129/year (full analytics, advanced features)

### Unique Features
- **Historical article search** - Find any article you've published instantly
- **Comment insights** - AI-summarized reader feedback
- **Competitor tracking** - Monitor other accounts (paid)

---

## 6. Native WeChat Editor (微信公众号原生编辑器) ⚡ Minimalist

**URL:** https://mp.weixin.qq.com/

### Strengths
- **Official platform** - No third-party risks
- **Zero learning curve** - Familiar interface
- **Instant preview** - Real-time phone preview
- **No copy-paste** - Already where you publish
- **Privacy** - No data sharing with external services

### Weaknesses
- **Extremely limited** - Basic text formatting only
- **No templates** - Must create everything from scratch
- **No advanced features** - No AI, analytics, or automation
- **Time-consuming** - Manual formatting for every element

### Best for
- Minimalists who value simplicity
- Privacy-conscious users
- Ultra-simple newsletters
- Users with strict security requirements

### Pricing
- Free (built into WeChat)

### Unique Features
- **Voice input** - Dictate articles on mobile
- **Native media library** - Direct access to your uploads
- **WeChat-specific tools** - Video card, product card insertion

## Choosing Your Tool: Decision Tree

**Start here:** What's your top priority?

### Priority: Save Money
→ **96 Editor** or **Native WeChat Editor**
- Both are completely free
- 96 Editor if you want some templates
- Native if you want absolute simplicity

### Priority: Beautiful Layouts
→ **Xiumi Editor**
- Best visual design tools
- Great for image-heavy content
- Modern aesthetic

### Priority: Maximum Features
→ **135 Editor** or **iPaiban**
- 135 Editor for templates + AI + convenience
- iPaiban for technical control + customization

### Priority: Workflow Efficiency
→ **Yiban Assistant**
- Works inside WeChat backend
- No copy-paste needed
- Built-in analytics

### Priority: Simplicity & Privacy
→ **Native WeChat Editor**
- Official, secure
- No third-party access
- Straightforward

## Pro Tips

1. **Don't marry your editor** - Try 2-3 options before committing to paid plans
2. **Save your templates** - Build a personal library of go-to styles in any editor
3. **Use browser bookmarks** - Quick access to your most-used tools
4. **Learn keyboard shortcuts** - Most editors support Ctrl+B, Ctrl+I, etc.
5. **Always preview on mobile** - WeChat's preview feature is your best friend
6. **Keep it simple** - Fancy editors ≠ better articles. Focus on content first.
