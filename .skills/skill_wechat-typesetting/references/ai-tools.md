### 2. Midjourney (Premium Quality)
**Access:** Requires Discord + paid subscription ($10-$60/month)

**Best for:**
- Ultra-realistic photography
- Artistic illustrations
- Brand-consistent visual series
- High-end aesthetic content

**Aspect ratio command:** `--ar 47:20` (equivalent to 900×383)

**Style reference feature:** Use `--sref <URL>` to maintain consistent style across all covers

**Example prompt:**
```
/imagine modern workspace with coffee and notebook,
morning sunlight, warm tones, minimal composition --ar 47:20 --v 6
```

---

### 3. DALL-E 3 (via ChatGPT)
**Access:** ChatGPT Plus subscription ($20/month)

**Best for:**
- Quick iterations
- Text rendering in images (better than most AI)
- Specific object combinations
- Cartoon/illustration style

**Tips:**
- Ask ChatGPT: "Generate a WeChat cover image (900×383) about [topic]"
- Be specific about text if needed: "Include the text 'New Product Launch' in bold"
- Request revisions: "Make it more colorful", "Remove the background person"

## AI Image Generation Best Practices

### Quality Prompts Include:
1. **Subject** - What's in the image
2. **Style** - Photorealistic, illustration, 3D, cartoon
3. **Mood/Lighting** - Bright, moody, warm, cool, dramatic
4. **Composition** - Close-up, wide shot, centered, rule of thirds
5. **Color scheme** - Vibrant, pastel, monochrome, your brand colors
6. **Technical specs** - Aspect ratio, resolution notes

### Example Quality Prompt:
```
A minimalist flat-lay photograph of a smartphone, notebook,
and coffee cup on a white marble desk, soft natural window light
from the left, pastel pink and blue accents, top-down view,
professional product photography style, clean and modern
```

### Avoid:
- Vague prompts: "Nice picture about technology"
- Too many subjects: "Person, dog, car, sunset, mountains, laptop..."
- Conflicting styles: "Realistic cartoon photo illustration"

## Copyright & Usage Notes

**AI-generated images:**
- Most platforms (Midjourney, DALL-E) allow commercial use with paid subscription
- Always check the specific platform's terms
- No attribution usually required, but verify

**Stock photos:**
- Read license carefully (commercial use, attribution requirements)
- Keep download receipts as proof of license
- Never use watermarked images

**WeChat-specific rules:**
- No misleading AI-generated "news" photos
- Disclose if AI image could be mistaken for real photo in news context
- Respect intellectual property (don't clone famous artworks)

## Quick Workflow for Cover Creation

1. **Define concept** - What's the article about? What emotion to convey?
2. **Choose tool** - Free (DeepSeek, Bing) or paid (Midjourney, DALL-E)
3. **Generate 3-5 options** - Iterate on prompts
4. **Select best** - Preview on mobile screen
5. **Edit** - Resize to 900×383, adjust brightness/contrast if needed
6. **Add text (optional)** - Use Canva/稿定设计 for overlaid title
7. **Test on WeChat** - Upload as draft, preview on phone

**Time estimate:** 10-20 minutes with AI tools vs. 30-60 minutes searching stock photos
